## F. Pacing rules measured
- A new visual event every **0.4–0.8 s**; a chrome word changes every **1.2–3 s** (one per idea); ribbed scene every **4–8 s**, 1.5–4 s long.
- Exactly ONE chrome word on screen at a time; captions and word leave together.
- Speaker beats rotate: chrome word + tags → chips/plate → behind-graphic (rays/wave/bars/gauge) → ribbed scene → back.
- Landing page once (after the hook); B-roll 1–2 times per 30 s.
- Their on-screen production rules (E04): never open on a black frame · headline 1–4 words · a headline stays ≤ 5 s · lines stack tight (10 px) · plates land on the spoken word · nothing outside the safe zone · sound lands on the word. Their bug list: word sits too high · plate covers the hand · gap between lines too big · start on black.
