## Look

(`#2fd2c0`, 6–10 px squares/dots, 40 of them drifting slowly, 60 % opacity), black condensed headline, **teal script-italic** subline,
real objects: polaroids, a torn receipt, a Premiere timeline card, phones. Clean, editorial, Apple-white.

Paper `#eceff1`, ink `#141718`, grey `#75787a`, grid `#c2cbcb`, mint `#2fd2c0` (accent text, particles, neon box), CTA black pill `#111` with "EDIT" in mint.

## Motion & sound
Particles drift up 0.4 px/frame. Elements drop in with paper-like softness (spring damping 18). Stamp: scale 1.5→1, shake 4 f, thump.
Polaroid: flip-in (rotateY 90→0). Receipt prints line by line (printer tick). Keep the AFTER video audio.

## the user's brand
This style is white by nature and he dislikes white cards. If he picks "My brand", say so and suggest style 5 (same layout, dark),
or keep the layout here with paper `#141414`, grid `#262626`, red `#E30A17` particles and accents, polaroid frames → frosted dark glass without borders.
