## Motion (10 s)
0–0.4 s bubble pops · 0.3–0.8 s tag + line · 0.6–1.0 s chrome headline lands (boom) + subline swipes in · 1.0 s phones slide up (BEFORE from left −6°, AFTER from right) ·
the AFTER phone plays the edit for the rest; the BEFORE plays raw, muted · prompt bar types at 1.2 s, send ↑ pulses · last 0.8 s: CTA pill pops / "Download → Saved".
Everything except the AFTER phone stays still after it lands (the edit is the star).

## Sound
Measured: the AFTER clip's own audio (voice + its few SFX) is the soundtrack; about half of the ads add a trending hip-hop/R&B song
at voice level on top. No pops/booms on the wrapper (it doesn't animate). If the user wants a song: duck it −8 dB under his voice.
