# 13 · Pastel SaaS Motion  (commercial product motion graphics)

Preview: `previews/13-pastel-saas.jpg`. Reference reels: DW8aAzkDYGW (8 s, "Emails everywhere / Messages Lost / Updates Buried / imagine everything in one place / Need a quick… / Tag the right person"),
DW8ztQIDVXs (same, inside a "New Direction · Motion Graphics · commercial video" frame).

## Look
A **white canvas with soft magenta-violet gradient blobs** that breathe and slide (`#fffbff`, `#f7e7f7`, `#e3cbe5`, `#cca9cf`, accent `#c145c8`),
**floating notification cards** (white, radius 14, soft shadow, Gmail/Slack-like icons, red badge dots `#ff3b30`, tiny grey text),
and a **condensed narrow sans** headline in a purple→black gradient that types with a blinking cursor. Clean B2B SaaS ad (problem → solution).

## Type
- Headline: Barlow Condensed 500–600 / Saira Condensed, 120–140 px, tracking −3 %, fill `linear-gradient(90deg,#c145c8 0%,#7a2a8a 55%,#2a1030 100%)` or solid `#1a0f1f` for the solution words.
- Two-size compositions: "Emails" big + "everywhere" smaller, lower baseline; "Messages Lost" with a red ❗ mark.
- Typing: 1 letter / 2 frames, a 6 px violet caret `#c145c8` blinking; words get "deleted" backwards for the turn.
- Small UI text: Inter 400 18–20 px `#666`.

## Elements
- **Notification storm**: 8–14 cards fly in from all sides at different depths (scale .6–1.1, blur by depth), drift and rotate ±4°, red badges pop.
- **Alert cards** ("New update · Alex uploaded Screenshot.png", "Emma changed task status to 'In Review'") with a red ⚠ icon.
- **Gradient wipe**: a huge violet blob sweeps across to change scene (the white gets swallowed).
- **Product reveal**: the app UI (or a pill toggle) appears in the middle of a violet field; "Tag the right **person**." with the key word in magenta.
- Diagonal **marquee band** variant: a teal strip "IMPRESS · BE REMEMBERED · IMPRESS…" scrolling at 8° (shared with style 14).

## Motion
Fast and light: cards with spring {damping 16, stiffness 220}, blobs move continuously (sin drift, 4–6 s periods), camera slow zoom-in.
Cuts on every phrase (≈1–1.5 s).

## Sound
Upbeat corporate-pop / future-bass at −12 dB, notification pings on cards (varied pitches), keyboard typing, a "swoosh-boom" on the reveal.

## Voice & music (measured on the references)
No voice at all: music + UI sounds only (0.1 hits/s outside the pings).

## Measured (DW8aAzkDYGW, 8.3 s, 16:9 1276×720)
Opens on a desktop collage of mail/chat apps → white canvas with breathing magenta-violet blobs → words type in a condensed sans with a
purple→black gradient and a magenta caret: "Emails" → "Emails everywhere" (two sizes) → "Messages" → "Messages **Lost**" → "Updates" →
"Updates **Buried**" → "N(eed a…)". Gmail-style notification cards (M icon, red badge, tiny grey text) spawn around the words at several
depths, drift, and scatter outward on "Lost"/"Buried". A huge violet blob sweeps across between phrases. Also exists as 9:16 inside a
"New Direction · Motion Graphics · commercial video" frame (DW8ztQIDVXs).
